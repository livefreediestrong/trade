How to use Tomahawk

A practice trading desk. It uses fake money unless you deliberately connect a real Alpaca account in Advanced.

  1. Set today's goal and your practice cash. Example: goal $50, cash $10,000.
  2. Choose how trades happen. Ask me first — every idea waits for you to Approve or Skip. Auto fill — the desk places practice trades on its own.
  3. Press Start checking. The desk looks at your watchlist every few minutes while the market is open (9:30 am – 4 pm Eastern, weekdays).

The big card shows the latest call and why. When an idea needs you it appears on that card — press A to review or S to skip (you get 5 seconds to undo). Press Stop any time; open positions keep their stop-loss, and you'll see how the day went.
