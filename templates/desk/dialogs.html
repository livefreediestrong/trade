How to use nadzeeɫ

nadzeeɫ opens on Overview, a read-only snapshot. Use the tabs along the top: Fox is your real broker account, Paper is practice with simulated money, and Ticket places an order you choose yourself.

  1. Open the Fox tab to check your live account and execution mode. It shows broker balances, positions and desk order history. Start checking starts research using the current live mode. In approve-each-order mode, review the account and order, then type the ticker before submitting.
  2. Open the Paper tab for simulations. Choose its paper risk preset and press Start paper research. Turn on Automatically approve eligible paper research to simulate eligible ideas without reviewing each one. These controls never enable automatic live trading.
  3. Review the correct account. Live and paper keep separate idea lists, latest calls and results. Hold decisions and stale quotes remain blocked. Paper auto approval never enables live automation.

Use the idea buttons to open an explicit review. Navigation shortcuts never trade or skip an idea. Stop stops new live research; Stop paper research stops new simulations. Neither closes existing positions. Orders from this desk have no attached protective exits; manage those at your broker.
