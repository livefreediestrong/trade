Company check

One stock, five quick reads: its bottom line, public insider disclosures, federal contracts, lobbying and Congress trades. Insider purchases, sales, grants and tax transactions are shown separately, with filing dates and original notes. Research only. Nothing here places, sizes or blocks a trade.

Type a symbol. A first check reads five public sources and can take about 20 seconds. Successful checks may be reused for up to 6 hours; incomplete sources are retried sooner. This is not a real-time insider feed.
