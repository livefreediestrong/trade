My routine & your goal

Runs while this app and PC are on. Skips weekends and exchange holidays. The selected model and its existing audits may incur their usual API costs. A profit goal never increases position size or loss limits.

Moss's access & activity

Can read: market history, watchlists, research records, scored outcomes, paper holdings, live account balances, holdings and actual execution reports. Can write: its local notebook, research records, statistical ranking weights and simulated paper fills when the paper workday is enabled. Actual broker trades are retained in a separate read-only review journal. Can use: your configured research model. No credentials are shown or placed in the notebook.

Moss cannot submit or cancel broker orders. Its rehearsal plan does not control the desk's separate legacy automatic mode.
