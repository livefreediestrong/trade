Evidence rules & adaptive sizing

Mock data, future or out-of-session ticks, stale execution, missing provenance and overlapping ticker horizons are excluded. Liquid stocks with higher measured completed-bar volatility receive research priority; rotation preserves coverage.

The optional empirical-Bayes personality pulls small cohorts toward a peer baseline. The discount tapers from 10 to 19 samples and is removed at 20. Paper size grows only with positive after-cost expectancy, stable win rates and a lower 95% Wilson win-rate bound above 50%. The maximum paper amount, position count, total exposure and daily-loss limits still apply.

Sample weight controls the blend of observed results and the baseline; it is not a capital allocation. Non-overlapping outcomes may still be correlated. The 20-sample rule is a research policy, not a pure Bayesian posterior or proof of future profit.

What can run automatically?

  • Stock paper research: market-session checks, candidate collection, quality filters, ranking and eligible simulated entries while the paper workday is enabled.
  • Research universe: daily US symbol-directory refresh; failed refreshes retain the last list and retry after an hour. Two to eight candidates per cycle keep requests bounded. Candidate coverage is not whole-market real-time monitoring.
  • Paper evaluation: horizon exits, optional pre-close exit attempts, outcome grading and end-of-day reports. Missing prices leave positions unresolved.
  • Actual trade journal: read-only broker execution and fee sync about once per minute while the app runs. Coverage depends on the Gateway history window.
  • Options: contract lookup, fresh price checks and fee/payoff calculations run on request. Paper entry and closing each require a review and click; automatic options trading is not implemented.

The PC, app and required data connections must stay available. These research features do not arm real-money trading.
