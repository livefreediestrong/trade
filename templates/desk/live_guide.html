Limit, market, filled — what do these mean?

Limit order
Your buy price is a maximum; your sell price is a minimum. It might not fill. DAY means the order expires at the end of that trading day if still unfilled.
Market order
Requests the next available execution price. The final price and fees may differ from your estimate.
Partial fill
Only part of the quantity traded. Check the remaining order before trying again or replacing it.
Sell or short?
Selling shares you hold reduces that holding. Selling more than you hold can create a short position; a sell idea is not automatically a position close.
