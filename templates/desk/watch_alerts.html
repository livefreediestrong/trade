Watchlist alerts

Get a desk alert when a stock you follow makes a big move, is about to report earnings, or has a high-impact headline. It covers the stocks Fox holds first, then your watchlist (up to 40), and checks every 5 minutes. Alerts only notify you; they never place, size or block a trade. If ALERT_WEBHOOK_URL is set in .env, they also go to Discord or Slack.
