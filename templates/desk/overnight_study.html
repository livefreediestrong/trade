How to read this experiment

“Reinvested” rolls each gain or loss into the next trade. “Same amount each trade” adds separate trade results using your original amount every time. Buy-and-hold makes one purchase and one sale over the same overall dates.

Historical prices include the provider's split and dividend adjustments. The opening prices are adjusted to the same basis for comparison; they are not executable order prices. The same fraction is invested on every trade. This does not simulate random missed fills, auction queues, whole shares, cash settlement, borrowing, taxes or earnings events.

The earlier/later split is a look back at already available history. A profitable result is an idea to test with future observations, not proof it will work. Changing Woman and Fox do not change live rules from this study.
