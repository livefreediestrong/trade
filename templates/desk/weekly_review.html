What “learning” means here

Evidence is saved separately from AI opinions. Current corrected observations take precedence over older copies. A weekly idea remains a suggestion until tested on later data. Missing days are unknown, not zero. Daily and weekly opinions do not count as extra evidence.

Uses the existing AI budget with a separate $0.25 estimated weekly-review ceiling on the day it runs. Runs while the app is open and the computer is awake; catches up on the latest missed week. Keeps 52 weeks. Past reports remain dated snapshots; future reviews use corrections. The language model’s weights, orders and live risk limits do not change.
