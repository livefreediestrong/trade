The maintenance plan, in plain language
  • Every five minutes: the existing Windows watchdog checks this desk. It can recover missing workers. An update restart requires an inactive session, verified empty broker holdings and orders, and no unresolved submissions.
  • Daily at 4:40 p.m., computer time: create and verify a backup first, then check databases and rotate large logs. Failed backups defer housekeeping. Source files and old archives stay for manual review. Tests of changed builds wait until the market is closed and the desk is healthy.
  • After the close: Changing Woman and Fox review the day after 30 minutes. Their cumulative weekly review runs 90 minutes after the week's final stock session. Early closes and holidays are included.
  • Before the next session: confirm Gateway sign-in, the correct account, fresh daily profit/loss, holdings, working orders and practical money limits. Missing information does not count as zero.
  • Weekly owner review: inspect the review and failures, check free disk space and choose an attended update window. Monthly, restore a backup into a separate test folder with network access blocked.

Gateway's own daily auto-restart is separate from the app watchdog. Weekly sign-in may still be required. The desk does not log Gateway out, automatically restore old orders, install updates or change trading limits.

Broker maintenance is planned downtime, not confirmation that a particular error is harmless. If a position is open, app-managed exits need this app and Gateway running; review protection at the broker before any restart.
