# nadzeeɫ design and research studio

Implemented from the user's Apache Trading App Logo Kit (4), principally Desktop App v3 and Brand Guide, and the September 23 Drive book review. The existing Flask/Jinja and vanilla JavaScript application remains in place.

## Design and companions

- `static/nadzeel.css`: design tokens, locally bundled Bodoni Moda / JetBrains Mono, desert sky bands, landscape, sidebar, cards and responsive overrides. Font licenses accompany the fonts. No font CDN is required at runtime.
- `static/nadzeel.js`: read-only projections of the existing `desk:state` event. `static/nadzeel_motion.js` / `.css` add the design package's animated landscape, mark, quill and recorded-trade constellation; see [Dynamic graphics](NADZEEL_DYNAMIC_GRAPHICS.md). The design kit's sample holdings and profits were not imported.
- `templates/desk/overview.html`: verified broker value, missing-P&L state, separate local-paper equity and next step. No interpolated account curve or invented return.
- `static/nadzeel-mark.svg`: static vector derived from the supplied mark's four-arm / six-step geometry. Bundled design-kit JavaScript was not executed or copied.
- `static/moss_avatar.js`: Fox (default), Changing Woman, or Both; follow the current section, stay by the notebook, or hide. Both use 4×4 atlases and observe/walk/read/rest poses. Characters travel to a named destination in the reserved margin, stop on arrival or while typing, suspend motion in hidden pages, and stay at the notebook on narrow screens or with reduced motion. Optional speech bubbles and trading steps are described in [Guided trading companion](GUIDED_TRADING_COMPANION.md).
- `static/changing-woman-atlas.png`: generated via the built-in image tool from the existing personal portrait-inspired avatar; 16 distinct frames. Original fox and original portrait assets are retained. Prompts and provenance: `docs/CHANGING_WOMAN_ASSET.md`.

## Research capabilities and boundaries

1. **Source library** (`research_library.py`, `research_sources.py`). Twelve reviewed documents have original summaries, document-derived years, Drive links, sections, intended horizon, claim type, limitations and extracted-text hashes. SQLite FTS searches summaries locally; it does not ingest or execute book code. Moss's notebook and paper-model context receive a bounded set of reference cards. Retrieved source knowledge is not a model weight update or measured strategy outcome.
2. **Stocks on Sale** (`research_studio.py`). One to twelve US exchange-listed equities/ETFs per request; adjusted daily closes, 60-session median, observed high, daily liquidity, complete-day relative volume and price stabilization. Requires at least 60 valid completed sessions and the latest completed US session. Provider adjusted closes include splits/dividends. SPY and optional sector comparisons require matching start/end sessions. Company references retain publication times and source links, cash-flow/leverage notes, decline explanations and valuation assumptions. These remain user-recorded, unverified references. No fair-value discount is fabricated. Daily signals do not establish an intraday entry.
3. **Execution quality** (`research_metrics.py`, retained `TradeJournal`). Capture quote evidence at signal intake and broker intent, only if actually present/fresh. Persist it in the journal for later analysis. Bid/ask midpoint slippage and last-trade differences are distinct; no later quote can become an earlier benchmark. Record size, type, session, fees, latency and linked observed fill fraction. Live and broker-paper groups and currencies stay separate. Existing quote providers may supply only a last trade; midpoint coverage then remains unavailable. Historical executions with no captured benchmark remain unmeasured. Order-wide missed opportunities require recorded intent and a later defined valuation point, so are not invented.
4. **Evaluation** (`research_metrics.py`). Four prespecified expanding chronological windows, training-only threshold choice, purged cross-boundary outcomes, retained attempts, source IDs, exact replay, no-filter and zero-interest cash observation baselines, confidence bins, and a deterministic whole-day cluster bootstrap of net expectancy. Five days is a minimum to display an exploratory interval, not proof of independence or edge. Missing/invalid confidence is excluded. Extra friction is additive to existing outcome cost assumptions. These observation summaries do not reconstruct portfolio equity or claim a full historical-market backtest.
5. **Frozen research protocols**. Source-linked hypotheses record trigger, available features, exit/horizon, costs, benchmark and invalidation. Optional prospective tracking tags future Moss paper observations before their outcomes. A setting/prompt change stops attribution. This observes the existing paper researcher: free-text strategies are not executed as code, and activating a protocol does not enable paper or live trading. Unlinked old results never become prospective evidence. Revisions create new immutable evidence records.
6. **Portfolio and options context**. Aggregate available signed USD equity marks by recorded sector; show linear uniform ±5/10/20% stress scenarios and missing/unsupported holdings. Historical correlations match adjusted return intervals, require at least 40 observations, and cover at most 12 held US symbols. Options scenario grids vary price, elapsed time and volatility for one to four same-expiry legs. European Black-Scholes, continuous dividend yield, user-supplied assumptions, and a standard 100-share multiplier. Theoretical value changes are not fill prices or trade P&L; early exercise, assignment, discrete dividends, fees, adjusted contracts and borrow are excluded. See [OIC option pricing](https://www.optionseducation.org/optionsoverview/options-pricing).
7. **Moss judgment evaluations**. Six frozen cases cover stale quotes, splits, missing statements, conflicting evidence, dependent small samples and a changed company outlook. Retain actual responses, model/prompt version, reviewer labels and available cost/latency. The deterministic scorer grades supplied labels; it does not pretend to verify prose independently or call a model. Empty cost remains unknown.

## API and persistence

`/api/research/` provides `library`, `sale`, `company-reference`, `hypotheses`, `prospective/start`, `prospective`, `evaluate`, `evaluate/<id>/replay`, `execution-quality`, `portfolio`, `correlations`, `options-scenarios`, `judgment-cases`, and `judgment-evaluation`.

All new endpoints inherit existing localhost Host/Origin protection. No new endpoint can place/cancel a broker order, arm trading, or change trading configuration. Research references, hypotheses, protocols and evaluations use immutable evidence records in the existing `evidence.sqlite3`; the FTS index is a separate rebuildable `research_library.sqlite3`. Paper, broker-paper and actual execution evidence remain identified separately.

The source-only release archive includes the local fonts, new character, modules and tests. Credentials and runtime data are excluded. Recovery uses the existing `release_tools.py` workflow; it never rewinds account or trading state.

## Validation

The full application suite passed 586 tests. The focused 208-test run exercised research quality/math/APIs, existing market/evidence behavior, companion/paper workday, broker lifecycle, execution repairs, live audit and workspace UI. The avatar behavior test exercises walking, reading, errors, hide, both/single selection, separate lanes, focus, hidden-page suspension, reduced motion and narrow screens. No real-money order was submitted or canceled during validation.

Browser interactions exercised source lookup, saved walk-forward evaluation/replay, option scenarios, stock screening and companion controls. Desktop and 390-pixel responsive layouts were inspected; narrow-screen horizontal overflow was zero. Read-only real historical requests returned adjusted US history; they do not establish execution readiness or fundamental valuation.
